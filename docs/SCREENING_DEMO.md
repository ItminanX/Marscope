## 40-second pitch (true for this build)
"Future explorers on Mars will plan every walk with data from many missions, scattered across separate archives.
MARSCOPE brings that evidence together around one thing: the route. Our pre-event prototype computes routes for
different mission priorities with transparent, exact routing, explains every segment, and turns the route into a crew
brief. In this version the terrain is a labelled synthetic test grid in the official MOLA format, so we show the
method, not Mars results. It's a planning aid, not a safety system — and it always shows its evidence."
