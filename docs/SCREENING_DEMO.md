## 40-second pitch
"Future explorers on Mars will plan every walk with data from many missions — topography, imagery, spectra — scattered across separate archives. MARSCOPE brings that evidence together around one thing: the route. Using NASA's MOLA laser-altimeter data for Jezero crater, it computes routes for different mission priorities — easier terrain, more science, or a balance — with transparent, deterministic routing. Click any segment and MARSCOPE explains why: the terrain, the data source, the method and its limits. Then it turns the route into a crew route brief. It's a planning aid, not a safety system — and it always shows its evidence."
